Mentor Vocab

Privacy Policy · Last updated: October 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Mentor Vocab Android app by Sojeeb Academy (package: com.sojeebacademy.mentorvocab). It explains what information the app handles, why, and the choices you have. Mentor Vocab works fully offline and without an account; signing in and cloud backup are optional. By using the app, you agree to this policy.
01

Information We Handle

Stored only on your device (in the app's local database and preferences):

  • Learning progress — word mastery levels, bookmarks, and words you have marked as read.
  • Practice and exam data — exam history, scores, and your weak words.
  • Daily activity — daily study activity, streak count, and the total time the app has been open.
  • Settings — theme, font size, daily goal, and reminder preference.

Only if you choose to sign in with Google:

  • Your Google account name, email address, profile photo, a unique account ID, and account creation / last sign-in times, provided through Google Sign-In and Firebase Authentication.

Only if you use Cloud Backup (requires sign-in):

  • A copy of your learning progress, exam history, daily activity, settings, streak, read-word marks, and total app-usage time.
  • The make and model of your device (for example, “Samsung SM-A15”) and the time of the last backup, so you can see which device made it.

We do not collect your contacts, location, photos, microphone input, advertising ID, or any payment information.

Account and cloud backup are optional
02

How We Use Information

  • To show your progress, streaks, statistics, and exam results inside the app.
  • To let you sign in and show your name and photo on your account page.
  • To back up and restore your progress across reinstalls and devices, when you turn that on.

We do not sell or rent your data, use it for advertising, or use it for profiling.

03

Cloud Backup & Sign-In

If you sign in, Mentor Vocab uses Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore, services provided by Google. Your backup is stored under your own account ID, and access is restricted to you by security rules. Backups can be started manually, or run automatically on a schedule if you enable automatic backup in the app. Data is sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

The word dictionary itself ships inside the app and is not uploaded.

You can delete your cloud backup at any time from My Account → Delete my cloud backup. Signing out stops further backups.

Your backup is tied only to your account
04

Permissions

  • Internet — used only for optional sign-in, optional cloud backup, and Google Play update checks.

Mentor Vocab does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, storage, or phone state.

05

Third-Party Services

The app relies on these Google services, which process data under their own privacy policies:

  • Google Sign-In / Credential Manager and Firebase Authentication — optional sign-in.
  • Cloud Firestore — optional cloud backup storage.
  • Google Play in-app updates — tells you when a new version is available.
  • Android Text-to-Speech — pronunciation is generated by the speech engine on your device.

See the Google Privacy Policy and Firebase Privacy Information. The app contains no advertising networks, no analytics or tracking SDKs, and no in-app purchases.

No ads, no trackers, no data selling
06

Data Retention & Security

On your device: data stays until you clear app data or uninstall the app, which permanently removes it.

In the cloud: a backup is kept until you delete it in the app, or ask us to delete it (see “Your Rights”). We use reasonable safeguards, but no method of storage or transmission is 100% secure.

07

Children's Privacy

Mentor Vocab is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has signed in or backed up data, contact us and we will delete it.
08

Your Rights

  • Use without an account — the app works fully without signing in.
  • Delete cloud data — use “Delete my cloud backup” in the app.
  • Delete your account data — email us from your sign-in address and we will remove your account and any remaining backup data.
  • Access or correct — email us and we will help. Users in the EEA and other regions keep all rights granted by their local data protection laws.
09

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will appear on this page with a new “Last updated” date. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the revised policy.
